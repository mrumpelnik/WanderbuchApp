iCloud Sync and Backups

Wanderbuch automatically uses Apple’s managed iCloud service to synchronize app-owned hike, hiking-spot, and spot-encounter records when iCloud is available. Local use continues when iCloud is unavailable. Synchronization is asynchronous and depends on your iCloud account, available storage, network access, and Apple’s services. Immediate delivery or identical data on every device at all times is not guaranteed.

Synchronization is not a separate backup: changes and deletions may propagate to your other devices. Deleting the app does not guarantee deletion of data already stored in private iCloud storage. Unsynchronized changes may be lost when changing iCloud accounts, removing iCloud data, or losing a device. Keep independent copies of important app records using the full JSON backup export in Settings > Backup & Export. These exports do not include original photos, raw HealthKit samples, or workout route payloads.
